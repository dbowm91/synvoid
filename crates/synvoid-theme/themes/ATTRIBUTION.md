# Bundled Halloy Theme Attribution

## Source

These 50 themes use the Halloy-compatible TOML format, originally designed for
the [Halloy IRC client](https://github.com/halloy-im/halloy). The files are
vendored from the upstream catalogue at <https://themes.halloy.chat/> and drive
SynVoid's WAF block/error pages only. The vendored copies carry no per-file
`[metadata]` sections.

## Credits

Each theme's original author is credited by the upstream catalogue. The themes
are community-contributed and redistributed under their respective licenses.
